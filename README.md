# 📊 AI Tool Usage Among College Students

### Statistical Analysis of AI Tool Usage Among College Students

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project studies the usage of Artificial Intelligence (AI) tools among college students through a primary survey.

The study explores how frequently students use AI tools, the time they spend using them, their main purposes, and their opinions about the usefulness of AI in academic activities.

The project applies statistical methods to analyze survey responses and present the findings through tables, charts, and statistical calculations.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- To study the frequency of AI tool usage among college students.
- To identify the AI tools most frequently used by students.
- To analyze the time spent using AI tools per day.
- To understand the main purposes of AI tool usage.
- To examine students' helpfulness and satisfaction ratings.
- To study the relationship between AI tool usage and students' perceptions.

---

## 📋 Dataset

- **Data source:** Primary survey using Google Forms.
- **Total responses:** 107.
- **Target population:** College students.
- **Data type:** Survey-based quantitative and categorical data.

Personal information has been removed from the publicly shared dataset.

## 📈 Statistical Methods Used

The project uses the following statistical techniques:

- Frequency distribution tables
- Measures of central tendency: Mean, Median, and Mode
- Measures of dispersion: Range, Variance, and Standard Deviation
- Quartiles, Deciles, and Percentiles
- Skewness and Kurtosis
- Histograms and Bar Charts
- Pie Charts and Scatter Plots
- Correlation Analysis
- Regression Analysis

## 🔍 Key Findings

- AI tools are used by students for various academic purposes.
- ChatGPT is the most frequently reported AI tool in the survey.
- Students reported different levels of AI usage and daily time spent.
- Satisfaction and helpfulness ratings were generally positive.
- The survey explores the relationship between students' perceptions of AI tools.
